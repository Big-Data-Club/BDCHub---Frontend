#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The user correctly identified a major architectural aesthetic defect: the student workspace ([learn/page.tsx](file:///home/thanh/BDCHub---Frontend/src/app/(learning)/lms/student/courses/[courseId]/learn/page.tsx)) suffers from **container-in-container syndrome** and **card overload**. Wrapping the primary learning workspace in a massive white card (`bg-white border rounded-2xl p-6 sm:p-8 shadow-sm`) inside an already-padded page container wastes up to 120px of horizontal space and creates a generic, boxed "AI prompt output" look rather than a modern, immersive learning workspace.

**Deterministic scan**: Detector confirmed 13 literal micro font-size overrides in analytics sub-views ([MasteryTab.tsx](file:///home/thanh/BDCHub---Frontend/src/components/lms/student/analytics/MasteryTab.tsx)).

#### Overall Impression
The interface relies heavily on decorative cards and heavy border-radius bubbles (`rounded-2xl`/`rounded-3xl`) as a crutch for visual grouping, resulting in artificial visual walls and poor space utilization.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Giant Outer Container & Double Padding Waste (`learn/page.tsx`)**
  - **Why it matters**: Wrapping the main content area in a giant `rounded-2xl p-6 sm:p-8` white card inside an already-padded container restricts effective width for PDFs/videos/quizzes and creates a template-like AI feel.
  - **Fix**: Remove the outer white card wrapper, borders, and heavy padding. Let the content layout breathe naturally across the full canvas width.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Systemic Card Overuse (Card Fatigue Across Workspace)**
  - **Why it matters**: Every single component (`PrevNextButtons`, status banners, stats sections) is wrapped in its own rounded box border with background fills.
  - **Fix**: Replace heavy card borders with clean visual hierarchy, subtle divider lines, and whitespace grouping.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Excessive Bubble Border Radius (`rounded-2xl`, `rounded-3xl`)**
  - **Why it matters**: Oversized border radii make technical learning content feel overly rounded and toy-like.
  - **Fix**: Tame border-radius tokens down to clean, professional radii (`rounded-lg`/`rounded-xl` for inputs/buttons, zero-radius/subtle borders for main workspace sections).
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Restricted Content Viewer Space**
  - **Why it matters**: PDFs and document viewports are squeezed inside nested card padding, forcing double scrollbars and reducing reading area.
  - **Fix**: Allow `ContentViewer` to stretch full-width within the workspace column without inner card padding constraints.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`
